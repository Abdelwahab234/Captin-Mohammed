A professional fitness coaching website for Coach Mohamed Hamed, specializing in weight loss, nutrition, body transformation, and fitness coaching. The website is designed to attract potential clients, showcase the coach’s expertise, and increase subscription conversions through a modern and engaging user experience.

The landing page features a powerful hero section with motivational messaging, professional fitness imagery, and strong call-to-action buttons. Interactive animated statistics highlight key achievements, including over 110,000 followers and 1,560+ successful clients. A dynamic timeline section showcases the coach’s services, expertise, and client benefits through scroll-based animations.

The platform includes detailed subscription plans, allowing visitors to compare packages and choose the option that best fits their goals. A dedicated subscription process section guides users through payment via InstaPay and submission of payment confirmation through WhatsApp with one-click contact buttons.

Additional sections include social media integration, client testimonials, transformation success stories, and a final conversion-focused call-to-action. The website follows a premium fitness brand aesthetic using Neon Green and Black as the primary colors, with smooth animations, responsive design, and mobile-first optimization.

Key Features:

Modern fitness-focused landing page
Responsive design for all devices
Animated counters and scroll-triggered effects
Interactive timeline showcasing services and expertise
Subscription plans and pricing section
WhatsApp and social media integration
Client testimonials and transformation stories
SEO-friendly structure
Built with React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion for high performance and scalability.
